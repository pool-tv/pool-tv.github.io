What is Pool TV?

Pool TV is a one-man television universe — created, hosted, written and performed by Sean. At the center of it is a masked host in red and black who travels through time, space and every channel on the dial, and no two stops are ever the same show.

Part comedy show, part brain-retraining, part magic and mischief — Pool TV bends the format until it breaks, then keeps broadcasting. Three seasons are out in the wild, and every episode, every joke and every detour through the static has been quietly setting up one grand finale.

And Pool TV doesn’t just make television. It builds its own video games, on its own game engine — the Pool Tv 🕳📺 Game Engine — Pool TV: No Signal and Rockstar are out front, and a third game is still classified.

Two games, both built on the Pool Tv 🕳📺 Game Engine — and a third one still classified. First up, the one you can play right now:

Pool TV: No Signal

Survive the static

Pool TV: No Signal is a movement-only arena-survival roguelike for Android and PC. You move — your weapons fire themselves. Pick from 21 fighters, fight across 15 channels, and whatever you do, be ready: a boss is waiting at 15:00 on every single channel.

It stars Mr. Pool himself — and hiding somewhere in the roster is PuppyBears, Sean’s dog, as a secret character. Clear a channel to unlock the next. Clear Death, and Endless mode is yours.
